# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 November 2026_

This policy explains how **Things to Do Next Planner** ("the app") for iOS, watchOS and Android
handles information. The app is developed by Robert Langat.

## Your tasks stay on your device

Everything you create in the app is stored **only on your device**: tasks, lists, notes,
subtasks, reminders and settings. The developer has no server that receives your tasks, and
there's no account to create.

## Optional features you control

- **Backup files.** If you export a backup, the file is saved where you choose (for example the
  Files app). Nothing is sent to us.
- **Google Drive sync (if enabled in your version).** If you sign in with Google, your tasks are
  stored in a private, hidden app folder in **your own** Google Drive (scope `drive.appdata`).
  The app can't see your other Drive files. Google's privacy policy applies to that storage. You
  can sign out at any time in Settings.
- **AI planner (if enabled in your version).** If you use the AI planner, the app shows you the
  exact text it will send (your planning request, the date and the available time) and sends it
  only after you approve. It goes to the planning service to create a suggested schedule and is
  not used for advertising.
- **Notifications** are scheduled locally on your device. Reminder content is not sent anywhere.

## Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from **Google AdMob**. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:
- device identifiers (on iOS, the Advertising Identifier, only if you allow tracking when asked)
- general location derived from your IP address
- information about ad interactions, plus diagnostic data

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), you'll be asked
for consent first. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Ads and privacy → Ad
privacy options**. On iOS you can also turn tracking off in **Settings → Privacy & Security →
Tracking**.

Your tasks are **never** shared with advertisers. Learn more about how Google uses data at
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

## Children

The app is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are
configured to show general-audience content.

## Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, deleting the app deletes it. If you used Google Drive
sync, you can remove the app's data in Google Drive under **Settings → Manage apps**.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
