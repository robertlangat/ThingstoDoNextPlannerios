# Support

Need help with **Things to Do Next Planner**? Report a problem or ask a question here:
**https://github.com/robertlangat/ThingstoDoNextPlannerios/issues**

## Common questions

**Reminders don't appear.** Open iPhone **Settings → Notifications → Things to Do Next** and allow
notifications. In the app, check that **Settings → Reminders** is on. Focus modes can also hold
notifications back.

**How do repeating tasks work?** When you edit or delete a repeating task, you can change just
that occurrence or that occurrence and all future ones. Monthly tasks on the 29th–31st fall on the
last day of shorter months. Yearly tasks on February 29 fall on February 28 in non-leap years.

**How do I back up my tasks?** Go to **Mine → Settings → Backup file → Export backup file** and
save it in Files. Use **Import backup file** to restore it. Importing merges with what's already
there; nothing is lost.

**How do I add the widget?** Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap **+**, search for "Things to Do
Next", and choose a size. Tap a circle on the widget to complete a task.

**Apple Watch.** Install the watch app from the Watch app on your iPhone. Tasks you tick on the
watch sync back to your phone. If the phone isn't nearby, they're queued until it is.

**Ads and privacy.** You can review or change ad consent in **Settings → Ads and privacy**. See
the [privacy policy](privacy-policy).
